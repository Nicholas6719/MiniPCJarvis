WHAT I SEE WRONG WITH IT
  1. The orb is not the centre. In panel mode it's demoted to a 300px column and the 88px nucleus reads as a decoration next to a 1200px wall of chat. Idle is the only time it's the subject.
  2. Eleven glyph tabs, one hover strip. ◈ ◎ ▣ ◐ ▤ ▦ ◍ ❖ ◔ ⌁ ⚙ are unlearnable, and they only appear when the cursor goes hunting near the top edge — bad for a voice-first machine you barely touch.
  3. Two type sizes doing all the work. 10–11px mono all-caps for every label and 13–15px Rajdhani for every body. No scale, so nothing is obviously more important than anything else.
  4. The 44px grid and the glow are constant. They read the same at idle and at fault, so the room never changes tone when something is actually wrong.
  5. State lives in three places. Orb label, status-bar dot, and the "doing…" line under the orb all say something slightly different at once.
  6. No sense of transition. Panels fade in over 160ms in place; the orb never moves. Nothing tells you the machine went from resting to working.
