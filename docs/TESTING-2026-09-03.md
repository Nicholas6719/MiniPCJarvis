## P1 — MINE, introduced today

**2. "Yes please go ahead and finish the render" → did a browser search.**
The overrun message asks "Shall I carry on?" but NOTHING IS LISTENING for the
answer — no pending confirmation is registered. So his yes was parsed as a
fresh utterance and routed. Asking a question nobody is waiting on is worse
than not asking. Compounded by `YES_WORDS` being deliberately strict
(`^yes$`-shaped), so even a registered confirmation would have missed
"yes please go ahead and finish the render".

**3. Asked the same question twice after being told no.** "Look at reddit and
tell me what's trending" → *"Did you mean news, sir?"* → no → repeated → same
question again. `_ask_if_unsure` drops the pending guess on a no but records
NOTHING, so the identical sentence scores identically and asks again. A
confirmation loop that cannot take no for an answer is a worse experience than
staying silent. **Must learn the negative.**
